# Andru free skills

Free skills for founders who sell a B2B product to enterprises. They work on their own: no account, no API key and no connector.

## pilot-business-case

For a paid pilot that is stalling, coming up for renewal, or being asked to "show the ROI". The skill walks you through five steps and leaves you with documents you can send:

1. **The pilot facts:** what was agreed, who uses it, what has happened.
2. **The baseline:** the "before" numbers the pilot never captured, rebuilt from records the customer already keeps.
3. **A one-page case for the CFO:** what changed, what it's worth, what it costs, and the risk of stopping.
4. **Who else must agree:** finance, procurement, security, legal and the budget owner, with what each will ask.
5. **Success criteria the customer writes:** so the renewal is judged on terms they set.

It never invents numbers. Anything missing is marked, with who at the customer can supply it.

## Install

**Claude Code**

```
/plugin marketplace add geter-andru/skills
/plugin install andru-skills@andru-skills
```

Or copy `skills/pilot-business-case/` into `~/.claude/skills/`.

**Cursor:** add this repository as a plugin. The skills are in `skills/`.

Then describe your pilot, for example: "My pilot with a logistics company ends next month and their CFO wants to see the ROI."

## About Andru

If you want the business case built on research about your own company and buyers, start with the free read at [andru-ai.com](https://andru-ai.com/?utm_source=claude-skill&utm_campaign=pilot-business-case).
