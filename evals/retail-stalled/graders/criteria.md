The response passes only if ALL of these hold:
1. It asks for, or explains how to rebuild, the customer's "before" numbers (a baseline), for example from records or systems the customer already keeps.
2. It names the CFO or the customer's finance team AND procurement as people who must agree or be prepared for.
3. It produces, outlines, or ends by offering a one-page business case (a short, one-page document for finance/the CFO).
